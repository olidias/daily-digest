ACSM 2026 Position Stand: Landmark Resistance Training Guidelines — First Update Since 2009
ACSM Aug 2026
Synthesising 137 systematic reviews from 30,000+ participants, the American College of Sports Medicine's first updated Position Stand in 17 years establishes that any resistance training beats none; ≥10 sets/muscle/week with loads from 30–100% 1RM are all effective for hypertrophy provided proximity to failure; ≥80% 1RM is the evidence-backed threshold for strength; training to momentary failure does not consistently improve outcomes for healthy adults; bodyweight and elastic-band protocols produce meaningful strength gains compared to no training.
