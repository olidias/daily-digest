  • ACSM Publishes First Resistance Training Position Stand Since 2009 — 137 Systematic Reviews
    ACSM2026
    Synthesising 137 systematic reviews covering 30,000+ healthy adults, the updated ACSM guidelines set goal-specific prescriptions: strength (≥80% 1RM, 2–3 sets, ≥2×/week), hypertrophy (≥10 sets/muscle/week, 30–100% 1RM), and power (30–70% 1RM, fast concentric); the headline finding is that training to momentary muscular failure confers no additional benefit over stopping 2–3 reps short of failure.
  • Meta-Analysis: 60–120 Min/Week Resistance Training Linked to 15% Lower All-Cause Mortality
    British Journal of Sports MedicineJun 2026
    A 30-year cohort meta-analysis found that 60–120 minutes of resistance training per week is associated with a 15% lower all-cause mortality risk and 17% lower cardiovascular disease risk, with peak benefit (~27% reduction in mortality) at approximately 60 minutes/week, after which marginal gains plateau.
