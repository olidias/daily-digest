Earth Reaches Aphelion Today — 152,087,774 km from the Sun

NASA Breaking News · 6 July 2026

At approximately 17:00 UTC today, Earth reaches aphelion—its annual farthest point from the Sun—at 152,087,774 km (1.0167 AU). Despite coinciding with Northern Hemisphere summer, aphelion has negligible temperature effect because axial tilt dominates seasonal heating by a far wider margin than the roughly 3% variation in solar distance across the year.
