JWST finds isolated supermassive black hole 700 million years after the Big Bang

Sky & Telescope Sep 2026

The James Webb Space Telescope has detected a "naked" supermassive black hole — roughly 50 million solar masses — with virtually no host galaxy around it just 700 million years after the Big Bang; the object, tentatively dubbed QSO1, confounds models in which black holes grow co-evolutionarily inside dense stellar environments and may represent an entirely new class of early-universe object.
