    Sentinel-3C Returns Its First Views of Earth

  • Astronomy Picture of the Day — 9 October 2026
    APOD: Stickney Crater on Phobos

    A colour-enhanced HiRISE image from NASA's Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter reveals Stickney Crater — nearly half the diameter of Mars' moon Phobos itself — in striking detail, showing grooves thought to have formed from the same catastrophic impact that nearly shattered the small moon.
