People Leadership

  • A New Way to Address Troubled Team Dynamics
    MIT Sloan Management Review 22 July 2026

    Ina Toegel and Jean-Louis Barsoux present a structured Big Five personality-framework methodology where teams complete assessments then participate in guided sessions that explicitly link collective trait profiles to existing interpersonal tensions; the same protocol accelerates cohesion in newly formed teams by surfacing fault lines before they harden into conflicts, giving leaders a replicable diagnostic rather than ad-hoc conflict resolution.

  • How Leaders Unlock Innovation on the Front Lines
    MIT Sloan Management Review 20 July 2026

    Field research by Mosner, Sting, and Chandrasekaran finds leadership behaviour—not worker motivation—is the primary bottleneck to front-line innovation; three levers identified: allocating time and budget for ideas, enabling low-stakes experimentation, and reinforcing employees' "innovation identities." Leaders who fail to signal openness cause workers to self-censor viable ideas.
