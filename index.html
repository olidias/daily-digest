Science-Based Lifting

ACSM 2026 Resistance Training Position Stand: First Major Update Since 2009

ACSM Apr 2026

137 systematic reviews (30,000+ participants) confirm: ≥80% 1RM for maximal strength; ≥10 sets/muscle/week for hypertrophy with loads from 30–100% 1RM all effective when sets are taken near failure; full ROM throughout; stopping 2–3 reps short of failure matches training-to-failure outcomes with less fatigue. Equipment type and complex periodisation are not critical variables.

ACSM/McMaster Longevity Guidelines: Any Resistance Training Beats None

2 Minute Medicine 2026

The landmark ACSM/McMaster collaboration finds the biggest longevity benefit comes from transitioning off sedentary behaviour to any resistance training; combined with aerobic exercise, the benefit extends further — confirmed by 30-year longitudinal data.

Stronger By Science: Modeling Strength Gains Over Time
