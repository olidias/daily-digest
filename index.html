August 12: Total Solar Eclipse + Perseid Peak Under New Moon — Rare Double-Header

NASA Science · Aug 2026

A total solar eclipse crosses northern Russia, Greenland, Iceland, and northern Spain on August 12 (first totality on the Spanish mainland since 1905), and the Perseid meteor shower peaks the same night under a new Moon — 50–70 meteors per hour in dark skies — making it one of the most observationally dense single days of the decade; a partial lunar eclipse follows August 27–28.

Jupiter-Mass Planet Found Orbiting a Brown Dwarf Orbiting a Star — Unusual Hierarchical Triple

Sky & Telescope · Jul–Aug 2026

Astronomers discovered a planet with roughly Jupiter’s mass orbiting a brown dwarf that in turn orbits a sun-like star, creating a three-body hierarchy that challenges current planet and brown-dwarf formation models and raises questions about dynamical stability over billion-year timescales.
