SpaceX Falcon 9 Upper Stage Impacts Moon Today at 06:35 UTC Near Einstein Crater

EarthSky / CGTN / TechTimes · 5 Aug 2026

A decommissioned Falcon 9 upper stage from a January 2025 dual-lander cargo mission (Firefly Blue Ghost & ispace Hakuto-R) struck the lunar surface at ~5,400 mph today near Einstein crater on the Moon’s western limb, generating a predicted 20–30 m crater and ejecta cloud that a Los Alamos team is using to test analytical methods for future lunar seismic networks and characterise debris-strike risks.

ISS Spacewalk 96 Tomorrow: Meir & Menon Exit at 08:35 ET to Install iROSA-7 Hardware

NASA · 5 Aug 2026

Flight engineers Jessica Meir (commander, her sixth EVA) and Anil Menon (first EVA) begin a ~6.5-hour spacewalk on Thursday to mount the structural baseplate for the station’s seventh iROSA roll-out solar array, the first of three planned August spacewalks that collectively aim to raise ISS electrical power output through its remaining operational years.

ESA Cluster Endgame: Samba and Tango Reenter South Pacific August 31 and September 1

SpaceNews / ESA · August 2026

ESA has maneuvered its final two Cluster magnetosphere-science spacecraft on staggered descents roughly 24 hours apart over the South Pacific; a Falcon 900 business jet equipped with 26 synchronised cameras across six instrument stations will observe each reentry, yielding fragmentation and demise data urgently needed to design future satellites for safer atmospheric disposal.

Total Solar Eclipse August 12 + Perseid Peak: Spain’s First Totality Since 1905

NASA Science / ESA · August 2026

Next Tuesday a total solar eclipse crosses Greenland, Iceland, and northern Spain (mainland Spain’s first totality since 1905) and a sliver of northeast Portugal; the Perseid meteor shower peaks the same night under New Moon conditions, with ESA and multiple national observatories instrumenting both events for corona science and upper-atmosphere meteor ablation data.

Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope Confirmed for August 30 Launch on Falcon Heavy

NASA · August 2026
