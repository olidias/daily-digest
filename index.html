Euclid Catalogues 31 Ancient Quasars; Two Set Redshift Records at z = 7.77 and z = 7.69

ESA / NASA Science · July 2026

The ESA-led Euclid wide-field survey identified 31 high-redshift quasars in a single imaging campaign, with the top two record-holders — EUCL J172902.75+641018.1 (z = 7.77) and EUCL J125308.55+705432.3 (z = 7.69) — both emitting light when the universe was under 670 million years old, surpassing the previous record-holder at z = 7.64 and offering new constraints on supermassive black-hole growth in the first billion years.

Quanta: Researchers Prove New Bounds on the Power of Quantum Proofs

Quanta Magazine · July 2026

Complexity theorists established tight new bounds on QMA(2) — the class of problems verifiable using two independent quantum witnesses — showing that entangled quantum proofs can solve problems far beyond those tractable with a single classical or quantum certificate, a result with implications for cryptography and quantum verification protocols.
